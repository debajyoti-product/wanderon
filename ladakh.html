Daily Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Leh | Acclimatization

Arrive at Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport in Leh. You'll be transferred to your hotel. Since you are arriving at an altitude of ~11,500 ft, today is strictly for rest and acclimatization. In the evening, you can take a gentle stroll to the Leh Market and Shanti Stupa to catch a beautiful sunset.

Where you'll stay

Standard / Premium Hotel in Leh (e.g., Hotel The Kaal or similar).

Leh City
Day 2

Leh Local Sightseeing | Sham Valley

Start your day with a hearty breakfast before heading out to explore the Sham Valley region. Visit the majestic Hall of Fame, experience the gravity-defying Magnetic Hill, and seek blessings at Gurudwara Pathar Sahib. Witness the stunning confluence of the Indus and Zanskar rivers at Sangam.

Where you'll stay

Standard / Premium Hotel in Leh.

Sham Valley
Day 3

Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La

Today we drive to the breathtaking Nubra Valley. En route, we conquer Khardung La, one of the highest motorable passes in the world (17,582 ft). Descend into the desert valley of Nubra, visit the Diskit Monastery, and enjoy a ride on the rare double-humped Bactrian camels at Hunder sand dunes.

Where you'll stay

Deluxe Swiss Tents in Nubra Valley. Enjoy dinner under the stars.

Nubra Valley
Day 4

Nubra to Pangong Tso

We take the offbeat and rugged Shyok River route to reach the magnificent Pangong Lake. Prepare to be mesmerized by the changing colors of the lake, nestled at 14,270 ft. Spend the evening walking by the shore and soaking in the surreal beauty.

Where you'll stay

Lakeside Swiss Camps at Pangong Tso.

Pangong Tso
Day 5

Pangong to Hanle via Rezang La

Wake up to a stunning sunrise over Pangong Lake. Today we drive along the lake towards the remote village of Hanle. En route, we visit the Rezang La War Memorial. Hanle is India's first Dark Sky Reserve, offering unparalleled stargazing opportunities.

Where you'll stay

Local Homestay / Guesthouse in Hanle. Authentic local hospitality.

Hanle
Day 6

Hanle to Umling La to Leh

The big day! We start early and drive to Umling La Pass, standing tall at an astonishing 19,024 ft, making it the highest motorable road globally. Celebrate this achievement, click pictures, and then begin the long drive back to Leh.

Where you'll stay

Standard / Premium Hotel in Leh.

Umling La
Day 7

Departure

After breakfast, we drop you off at the Leh Airport. Fly out with epic memories, a camera full of breathtaking landscapes, and a heart full of joy.
